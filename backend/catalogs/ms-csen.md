# MS in Computer Science and Engineering (MS-CSEN)

Program ID: MS-CSEN
Program name: MS Computer Science and Engineering
Degree type: Master of Science
Department: Computer Science and Engineering
Source: Santa Clara University Graduate Engineering Bulletin (Chapters 2, 6, 10, 13)

## Unit requirements

Students must complete a minimum of 46 units of graduate coursework to earn the MS in Computer Science and Engineering.

- At least 36 of the 46 units must be graduate-level CSEN (Computer Science and Engineering) units.
- At least 8 units must be CSEN graduate electives, beyond the core courses.
- The remaining units may be graduate electives from other departments, subject to the caps below.

## Core courses

All MS-CSEN students must complete the following graduate core courses:

- CSEN 210
- CSEN 279
- CSEN 283

## Non-CSEN electives

Graduate courses from other School of Engineering departments may count toward the 46-unit total as non-CSEN electives.

- Engineering Management (EMGT) courses are capped: a maximum of 6 units of EMGT courses may be applied toward the MS-CSEN degree. Cross-listed courses whose primary code is EMGT (for example EMGT 269, also listed as ENGR 269) count toward this cap.

## Graduate Core Enrichment Experience

The Enrichment Experience is mandatory for all MS students in the School of Engineering. It cannot be waived or substituted. It is a separate 8-unit requirement that is layered on top of the program units, not part of the 46.

- Students must complete 8 units of Enrichment Experience coursework.
- At least 4 of those units must come from Enrichment core courses.
- Coursework must span at least 2 of the following areas:
  - Emerging Topics in Engineering
  - Engineering and Business/Entrepreneurship
  - Engineering and Society
